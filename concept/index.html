History · not Scripture · c. 1526–1536
Tyndale: the Word from Greek into English, at the cost of his life
William Tyndale translated the New Testament straight from Greek into English. It was smuggled into England in 1526 and banned at once. He was executed at Vilvoorde, near Brussels, in 1536.

After the Word ▾

Sound, as proposed: the history text itself is never turned into music, because only Scripture takes form under the Law. What sounds is the tied verse (here Psalm 19:7), scored by the same Law as anywhere in the Bible and ending V–I on the home note. The light follows that verse.
