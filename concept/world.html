Sonolumina · concept

The Word as a world you can move through

Genesis 1

Drag to turn · pinch or scroll to come closer · tap a light to name it. Silent mockup: no sound yet.

What you are seeing

  • Each light is one chapter. There are 1,189 of them, from Genesis 1 to Revelation 22, in canon order.
  • Each light's height is that chapter's number of verses. Psalm 119 (176 verses) is the tallest, and Psalm 117 (2 verses) the smallest.
  • Its colour is the seasons strip's colour (variant B): blue for the most sorrowful chapters, gold for the most joyful.
  • The ring of brighter lights at the base marks where each of the 66 books begins.
  • Past Revelation, faint white lights carry on outward: the 20 approved After the Word entries. They are dimmer because they are history, not Scripture.

All of this comes from the app's own data: the verse counts, the stored feelings, and the history file. Nothing here is drawn by hand. The shapes (a spiral, a road) are design choices.

Two shapes to choose from

A · The spiral

Genesis sits at the centre, and the Bible winds outward, chapter by chapter, like a galaxy. You see the whole Word at once, and the music would travel the arm as it plays.

B · The road

The chapters stand like lamps on both sides of a road, and you walk forward through them. You see less at once, but it feels like a journey from the beginning to the end.

What the built version would add

  • It moves with the music. The current chapter's light rises, and each verse lights as it is sung.
  • The cross-reference links the app already plays would show as arcs of light between chapters.
  • It would be a third view beside the reader, never a replacement. On weak phones it would fall back to the strip.

Your choices

  • Shape A or shape B (or both, with a switch).
  • How it is drawn. This mockup uses the phone's plain canvas, so nothing is installed. A 3D library such as Three.js would give real depth and glow, but it is loaded from a public CDN, and your rule is no new installs. I'd keep the plain canvas unless you say otherwise.
