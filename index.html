Here Scripture ends. What follows is history, not Scripture.
1,189 chapters, Genesis to Revelation, each ranked against the rest: blue most sorrowful, gold most joyful
Each light is one chapter, Genesis to Revelation. Its height is the number of verses and its colour is the strip's: blue for the most sorrowful, gold for the most joyful. Bright dots at the base mark where each book begins. Faint white lights past Revelation are history, not Scripture. While the music plays, the chapter that is sounding rises with its verses. Drag to turn, pinch to come closer, tap a light to name it.
