Open Home to begin the dwelling. Together they gather fallen branches and loose river stones and raise the house in five stages. They work in daylight, rest at night, and keep the seventh day as rest.

The Building scale shows the house from outside. The Home scale looks inside.

Sound & light. The card at the bottom shows the Scripture for this moment and the verses read with it. The music and the motes of light follow what those words are about: water, light, growth, work, rest, the home. Tap ♪ for sound. Tap the verse to read it in full.

Daily life. Open Day to lean the day toward tending, building, or rest. Work stays between 07:00 and 18:00, and it cannot be scheduled on the seventh day. They share three meals — dawn, noon, and evening — fruit from the trees, at the hearth. They carry water from the river, clear the paths, and plant beds that grow over several days. A small herd of deer can be approached slowly, fed, and named. On the seventh day all work stops, the garden grows quiet, and they walk together and sit by the river.
